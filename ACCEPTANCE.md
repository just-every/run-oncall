# What you can do

The target is a complete US-first on-call and incident-response product: real independent phone/SMS/push paging, durable escalation, correct coverage, native mobile response, monitoring/chat/phone integrations, recovery and post-incident follow-through, plus scoped agents. CONTRACT.md defines the destination, not a claim that it ships today.

Current release 1 supports local/hosted coordination beside an existing pager: members/services, copied runbooks, incidents, alerts/replay, owned actions/reviews, named-zone primary/backup rotations/overrides, private ICS, reviewed Opsgenie/PagerDuty file move-in and atomic checkpoints/handoffs. Production paging remains OFF and the existing pager authoritative. Independent standby is not required for coordination; release 2 paging, mobile and standby remain unbuilt. Demo simulations never prove delivery.

OC-003 and OC-006 follow the deliberate 21 October 2026 policies in CONTRACT.md, authorized by the OC-053 brief: scoped machine clients may coordinate granted services through `/api/v1`, and explicitly approved recipients may accept minimal signed response events. Machine attribution never establishes a human session, human review or human acknowledgment. Existing member roles, protected export, paging OFF and the customer’s pager remain unchanged. The runtime, transfer/checkpoint and 1440/390 settings journeys named under OC-053 must prove scope, revocation, atomic audit/outbox commit, signed receipts, uncertainty, protected replay and inactive restore before completion. `tests/automation.mjs` exercises creator demotion/removal independently for origin, endpoint and receiving client, including next-request refusal and restart, and explicit commander/assignee and machine timeline/audit attribution. These are recorded meaning changes; their CHANGELOG bullets and RELEASE.json `contractChanges` records belong to the next release, with no issued-envelope edit. These amendments add no OC-003 or OC-006 acceptance point and record no OC-003 or OC-006 status change. The OC-006 journey runs with no approved endpoint. Not proved: live receiver/TLS interoperability, hosted scheduling latency, provider delivery, human response or paging readiness.
